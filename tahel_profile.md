## 11. Career Story & Personal Background

Tahel's interest in sales started at home. Both her parents worked independently — her mother runs a cosmetics store and works as a beautician; her father had a natural herbal store in Jerusalem. Growing up watching them build their businesses gave her an early fascination with how to grow revenue and bring in clients. Sales wasn't a career she fell into — it was something she was drawn to from the beginning.

After finishing her IDF service, she made a deliberate decision to challenge herself by moving to Canada alone. She started in in-person mall sales — approaching cold strangers, handing out samples, and closing cosmetics deals on the spot. She worked 12–14 hour days on her feet, collecting far more rejections than yeses. It was a grind, and it built something in her: the belief that a yes is always coming as long as you stay persistent and consistent. She was eventually promoted to lead a team of 5 before COVID brought her back to Israel in 2021.

Back in Israel during COVID, with limited hiring happening, she took a B2C sales role at A.A. Financial — cold calling prospects about tax refunds, managing the full cycle from close to fund recovery. She consistently converted 300+ accounts per month. After about a year, she felt she'd hit her ceiling and wanted more.

She had always been interested in marketing, so AdMaven — a pioneer in the AdTech space working with publishers and advertisers — felt like the right move. It was. AdMaven gave her the analytical layer her career had been missing: monetization strategy, traffic performance, CRM discipline, data-reading, optimization. She became fluent in the publisher side of the programmatic ecosystem, and she's been the top revenue generator in her department for three consecutive quarters while managing ~300 accounts and ~$500K in revenue.

She is now ready for the next step: a senior role at a larger, more established company where she can bring that same competitive energy to a bigger playing field.
