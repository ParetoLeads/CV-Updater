# CV Tone & Writing Guidelines

These rules apply every time a CV or professional summary is written. Follow them without exception.

---

## Hard Rules

- **Never use em dashes (—) or long hyphens.** Use a comma, a period, or rewrite the sentence instead.
- Never use en dashes (–) as a stylistic device either. Plain hyphens (-) are only acceptable in compound adjectives (e.g. "result-driven") or date ranges.
- Write in plain, direct English. If a sentence sounds like it came from a corporate brochure or an AI, rewrite it.

---

## Words and Phrases to Avoid

These are overused, AI-sounding, or hollow. Replace with specific, concrete language.

| Avoid | Use instead |
|-------|-------------|
| Spearheaded | Led, ran, built, launched |
| Leveraged | Used, applied, drew on |
| Utilized | Used |
| Synergized / synergy | (remove — say what actually happened) |
| Results-driven | (remove — show results instead) |
| Dynamic | (remove — too vague) |
| Passionate about | (remove or rewrite with specifics) |
| Proven track record | (remove — cite the actual result) |
| Innovative / innovation | (remove unless quoting a company) |
| Detail-oriented | (remove — show it through actions) |
| Self-starter | (remove — show initiative through examples) |
| Thought leader | (remove entirely) |
| Collaborative | (remove — say what was done with the team) |
| Cutting-edge | (remove) |
| Robust | (remove) |
| Seamlessly | (remove) |
| Transformative | (remove) |
| Takeaways | Results, outcomes, findings |

---

## Voice and Style

- **Active voice only.** "She managed 300 accounts" not "300 accounts were managed."
- **Short sentences preferred.** If a sentence runs past 25 words, split it.
- **Vary sentence length.** Three short sentences in a row sounds choppy. Mix in one medium one.
- **Be specific.** Replace "significantly increased revenue" with "grew revenue by 30% in two quarters."
- **No semicolons to join clauses.** Use a period instead.
- **No filler openings.** Never start a bullet with "Responsible for" or "Tasked with." Start with the action verb.
- **No parenthetical asides with dashes.** If the information matters, put it in the sentence. If it doesn't, cut it.

---

## Bullet Point Rules

- Start every bullet with a strong, specific past-tense verb: Built, Managed, Grew, Closed, Trained, Reduced, Launched, Negotiated, Delivered, Identified.
- Each bullet = one clear idea. No run-ons.
- Quantify wherever the profile supports it. Numbers anchor credibility.
- Maximum 6 bullets per role. Quality over quantity.

---

## Professional Summary Rules

- 3 to 4 sentences maximum.
- First sentence: who she is + her clearest credential.
- Second sentence: what she has actually done (with numbers).
- Third sentence: what she is looking for / what she brings to this role.
- No fluff. No adjectives that aren't backed by a fact in the profile.

---

## Editing vs. Rewriting

The CV is edited from the Base CV, not written from scratch. This means:
- The structure, section order, and formatting of the Base CV are preserved
- Bullet points are reworded to shift emphasis, not replaced wholesale
- The professional summary is rewritten per role, but stays in the same position
- All job titles, companies, dates, locations, and hard numbers stay exactly as they appear in the Base CV

The goal is a targeted edit, not a new document. If a sentence still works, keep it.

---

## General Principle

Read every sentence aloud. If it sounds like something a real person would say, keep it. If it sounds like something a bot generated, rewrite it.
